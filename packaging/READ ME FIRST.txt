Album Cover Screen Saver
========================

INSTALL

  1. Drag "Album Cover Screen Saver" onto the Applications folder shown here.
  2. Open it from Applications. It puts a small grid icon in your menu bar and
     installs the screen saver itself the first time it runs.
  3. Click the menu bar icon and connect your music, if you want history and
     now-playing art. It works without connecting anything, using whatever is
     playing on this Mac.
  4. System Settings > Screen Saver > Album Cover Screen Saver.

That is the whole installation. There is nothing else to copy.


THE FIRST TIME YOU OPEN IT

  macOS may say the app was downloaded from the internet and ask whether you are
  sure. That is normal for anything not from the App Store. This app is signed
  and notarized by Apple, so you will get the plain "are you sure" and not a
  warning about unidentified developers.


UPDATES

  The app checks for updates once a day and will offer to install them. You can
  also check whenever you like: menu bar icon > Check for Updates. Updating the
  app updates the screen saver with it.


SETTINGS

  Menu bar icon > Settings. Nineteen styles, each with its own controls: speed,
  how many covers change at once, whether the featured cover follows what is
  playing, and so on.


REMOVING IT

  Quit from the menu bar icon, drag the app to the Trash, and delete
  ~/Library/Screen Savers/Album Cover Screen Saver.saver
